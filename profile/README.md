## 01 / THE PROBLEM

### Software deserves better engineering.

Modern software is becoming increasingly complex.

Requirements are misunderstood. Verification is fragmented. Test failures are difficult to investigate. Engineering evidence is scattered across disconnected tools.

Teams are expected to deliver reliable software while operating with incomplete information about what was intended, what was tested, and what actually happened.

We believe this needs to change.

**QINIS exists to close the gap between software intent, verification, and engineering evidence.**

---

## 02 / WHAT WE BELIEVE

We believe that:

- Software quality should be engineered, not assumed.
- Requirements should be understood before implementation.
- Verification should produce meaningful evidence.
- Failures should create knowledge, not just tickets.
- Engineering decisions should be traceable and explainable.
- Quality engineering should be integrated into the software development lifecycle.

Our approach is simple:

**Understand what should happen. Verify what actually happens. Preserve the evidence. Improve engineering decisions.**

## 03 / OUR PRODUCTS

We are building an interconnected ecosystem of engineering products and infrastructure.

### PRISM

**THE INTENT REVIEW**

Understand software requirements before they become software problems.

PRISM helps engineering teams identify ambiguity, contradictions, missing scenarios, and unclear expectations in software requirements.

Its purpose is to make software intent explicit, reviewable, and verifiable.

`REQUIREMENTS` / `INTENT` / `AMBIGUITY` / `VERIFICATION CONDITIONS`

---

### TRACE

**VERIFICATION INTELLIGENCE**

Understand what happened when software was tested.

TRACE transforms test execution results and CI evidence into structured engineering intelligence.

It helps teams investigate failures, understand verification outcomes, and build historical knowledge about software behaviour.

`TEST EVIDENCE` / `FAILURE INVESTIGATION` / `CI INTELLIGENCE`

---

### QEP

**QUALITY EVIDENCE PROTOCOL**

A structured foundation for trustworthy quality evidence.

QEP defines how engineering verification evidence is represented, recorded, connected, and interpreted across systems.

`EVIDENCE` / `PROVENANCE` / `STANDARDISATION` / `TRACEABILITY`

---

### OMNI

**ENGINEERING KNOWLEDGE INFRASTRUCTURE**

Connect engineering knowledge, relationships, and evidence.

OMNI provides the foundation for connecting requirements, verification conditions, test executions, failures, and engineering decisions.

`KNOWLEDGE` / `RELATIONSHIPS` / `CONTEXT` / `INTELLIGENCE`

## 05 / HOW WE BUILD

We approach engineering through a continuous cycle:

**UNDERSTAND → DEFINE → BUILD → VERIFY → LEARN**

Every product should have:

1. A clearly defined problem.
2. Explicit requirements and acceptance conditions.
3. Documented architectural decisions.
4. Automated verification.
5. Traceable changes.
6. Meaningful release documentation.

We prefer small, verifiable improvements over large, unvalidated changes.
